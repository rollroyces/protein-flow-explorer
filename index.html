    About Protein Flow Explorer

    Protein Flow Explorer is a free, open tool for visualising, measuring and explaining 3D protein structures. Originally built for teaching — now extended for working researchers with sequence navigation, atom picking, UniProt / AlphaFold lookup, structure comparison, and shareable views.

    For students

    • Controls. Switch representations (cartoon / surface / sticks) and colour schemes (chain / SS / hydrophobicity / …).
    • Guided Tour. Scripted step-by-step walkthroughs for the curated proteins.
    • Trajectory. Play back a conformational change (hemoglobin T→R, kinase open→closed, …).

    For researchers

    • Measure. Click atoms in the 3D view → distances (Å), angles (°), dihedrals (°). Up to four picks at once.
    • Sequence. One-click navigation between sequence and structure. Click any residue to highlight it; click again to zoom.
    • UniProt lookup. Type TP53 or P04637 → loads the matching PDB or AlphaFold model.
    • Compare. Superimpose two structures on Cα and colour by per-residue RMSD.
    • Share link. The current view (protein, state, style, colour, tour step, measurements) is encoded in the URL — paste a link to reproduce the exact view.

    Data sources

    • Structures: RCSB PDB, AlphaFold DB
    • Identifiers: UniProt

    Educational tool, not for clinical use. Structure coordinates are fetched live; an internet connection is required the first time a protein is opened.
